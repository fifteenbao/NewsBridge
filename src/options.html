    Reading messages

    Messages go to your configured Feishu conversation. Sending a reminder does not mark its items as read. Unread items are included again the next day.

    The server sends unread reminders daily at 10:00 Asia/Shanghai, even when Chrome is closed. No minimum number of items is required.
